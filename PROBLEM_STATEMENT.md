# Hackathon Problem Statement: FinTech – Asset & Capital Management / Optimization Controls

## Context & Problem Statement

Financial institutions manage complex balance sheets across diverse asset classes, tight liquidity requirements, and rapidly shifting market conditions. When markets become volatile, traditional manual rebalancing and static risk controls break down—leading to delayed execution, sub-optimal capital utilization, and unexpected exposure to risk.

**The Challenge:**
Design and build an automated capital management or optimization tool capable of helping companies balance asset allocation while enforcing risk control.

---

## What You Need to Solve

1. **Optimization Strategy**: How should capital be allocated across assets to maximize risk-adjusted returns while adhering to real-world capital or liquidity constraints?
2. **Control & Safeguard System**: How does your system detect, prevent, or automatically respond to risk threshold breaches or market shocks in real time?
3. **Decision Dashboard**: How can financial officers and risk managers visualize market exposure, understand automated system decisions, and run hypothetical scenarios?

---

## Room for Innovation & Discovery

We are intentionally not prescribing specific models, regulatory formulas, or technical architectures. It is up to your team to decide:

- Which models, frameworks, or algorithmic approaches best suit the problem.
- What key risk metrics, compliance indicators, or stress factors your control engine should monitor.
- How your system handles dynamic rebalancing without incurring transaction penalties.

---

## Submission Guidelines & Deliverables

- **Working Application / Engine**: Core backend logic for portfolio optimization and safety controls.
- **Interactive UI / Dashboard**: Visualization tool for risk managers to monitor allocations, alerts, and system actions.
- **Repository & System Architecture**: Clean codebase with documentation explaining your financial logic, design trade-offs, and how to run the project.

---

## Evaluation Criteria

| Focus Area                        | Weight  | What We Look For                                                                               |
| :-------------------------------- | :------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Financial & Control Logic**     | **35%** | Soundness and depth of your risk controls, optimization math, and dynamic constraints.         |
| **Technical Architecture**        | **30%** | Code efficiency, modular design, ability to handle dynamic data, and overall system execution. |
| **User Experience & Clarity**     | **20%** | Clear visualization of allocations, transparent alert triggers, and actionable insights.       |
| **Innovation & Problem Approach** | **15%** | Novel approaches to risk detection, scenario testing, or automated intervention.               |
